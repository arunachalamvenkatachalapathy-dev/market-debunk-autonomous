Arun: Hold on. Crashed when? On which market? Show me the dated price, currency and unit, not just a dramatic red arrow.
Liya: But the chart is down.
Arun: Down from which starting point? A one-day fall and a one-year return tell different stories. And a futures chart is not the same thing as the price your local dealer will pay for your silver. The buy-sell spread matters.
Liya: So the headline isn't a trading plan.
Arun: Right. Check the timestamp and the actual price source, then ask what the trade would cost and what loss you can bear. Silver can move both ways. Follow Market Debunk, and share this with someone who trades from headlines.
