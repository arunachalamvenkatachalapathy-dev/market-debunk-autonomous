Liya: This gold bangle looks so wide. The reel calls it "5D hard gold" or "hollow gold". Is it more gold for the same money?
Arun: The outside size cannot answer that. If a piece is hollow, its shape can look bigger without weighing as much. We need the actual gold weight, not a guess from a photo.
Liya: What do I ask at the counter?
Arun: First, the weight and purity of the gold. Then the making charges and the total payable amount. For hallmarked jewellery, ask for the HUID and verify it in the BIS Care app. The invoice should separately show the item's description, net precious-metal weight, purity and hallmarking charge.
Liya: But the showroom says the larger design is a better investment.
Arun: Jewellery can be beautiful, but do not equate size with resale value. Ask the store in writing how it treats making charges, buyback and exchange. Compare two pieces with the same purity and their net gold weight and total bill. The answer may differ by design and store.
Liya: So the name "5D" is not the test.
Arun: Right. The bill and the buyback terms are. Follow Market Debunk, and share this with someone shopping for gold.
