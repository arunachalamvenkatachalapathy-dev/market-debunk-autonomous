Rank 003: "Check this before buying property". Original scenario and guidance, not a representation of the source Reel's spoken claim. Applicable to new projects; state regulatory portals differ and exemptions exist. MahaRERA primary buyer guidance explicitly recommends project search, title certificate, litigations, encumbrances, comparing draft and offered agreements, and checking survey/plot/CTS, address, unit number, carpet area and possession date. Source: https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/buying-property . Search for project status/complaints is on the same live portal. Do not imply all property in India must be registered under RERA, or that RERA registration guarantees title/returns. Seek local legal review.
