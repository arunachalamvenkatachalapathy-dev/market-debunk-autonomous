You walk into a new apartment. The lights are on, and the sales brochure says: "Move in soon." But what does the paperwork say?

Before paying a booking amount, find the project on your state's official RERA website. Check its registration and status, the promoter's disclosures, the promised completion date, and any complaints. Do not stop at a registration number in an ad.

For a Maharashtra project, MahaRERA says to examine the legal title certificate, litigations and encumbrances, and compare the draft agreement with the copy you are asked to sign. Check that the unit number, carpet area, possession date and plot details actually match.

A model flat can show you the dream. The papers tell you which unit and promise you are paying for. Get local legal advice before signing or transferring a large sum.
