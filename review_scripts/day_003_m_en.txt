An online creator shows a payout screenshot. The comment says: "Guaranteed returns. Message me for the link." In a minute, you're one tap from sending money.

Freeze the screen. A payout screenshot is not proof of where the money came from. Ask who actually holds the funds, what the product is, what fees apply, and where withdrawals come from. If the answer is vague, do not send a test deposit just to find out.

India's securities regulator warns about assured high returns, unregistered sellers, missing documents and pressure to act now. Verify the seller's registration through an official regulator source, read the actual documents, and check the payment recipient independently.

This does not label any particular creator a fraud. It is a check before your money moves.
