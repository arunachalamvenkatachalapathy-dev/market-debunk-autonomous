Liya: The reel says, "Come trade with me." It sounds like a shortcut.
Arun: A trading invite is not a risk report. First, what market is it talking about: shares, or futures and options? Those are not interchangeable.
Liya: Let's say futures and options. What would you check?
Arun: On an illustration of a ten-thousand-rupee position, a two percent gross gain is two hundred rupees. Subtract brokerage, taxes and other trading costs, and the net gain is less. But if the price moves against you, that loss is still yours. Do not turn that illustration into a promise.
Liya: Is the risk really that widespread?
Arun: A regulator study found 93 percent of individual equity futures-and-options traders lost money over the three financial years from 2022 to 2024. That is about that group and period, not all investors. Read the contract note and charges before you trade. Follow Market Debunk, and share this with someone tempted by a trading invite.
