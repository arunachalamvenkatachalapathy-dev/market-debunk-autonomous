Liya: Ex-showroom price, registration and tax, insurance, accessories, and some add-on packages. It is one big number.
Arun: Ask for each line separately. Registration and taxes are not the same as optional extras. Compare the insurance cover and price, and decide whether you want the accessories. This is not an accusation against this dealer; it is how to see what you are paying for.
Liya: What if I use a car loan?
Arun: Ask the lender for the Key Facts Statement before signing. For a new retail term loan from a regulated lender, it sets out the annual percentage rate, charges, and repayment schedule. A low EMI can hide a longer repayment period. Compare total repayment, not only the monthly amount.
Liya: So the check is an itemized on-road quote, and the lender's Key Facts Statement.
Arun: Exactly. Keep the written quote and ask about anything that changed before you sign. Follow Market Debunk, and share this with someone shopping for a car.
