At a Dubai ATM, you ask for dirhams. The screen then offers to charge your card in rupees. It feels helpful: you see a familiar number before you tap. But that choice is dynamic currency conversion, or DCC. The ATM's conversion provider sets the rupee rate, and may add a markup. A zero-forex card does not erase that separate conversion cost. Look for the local amount, the rupee amount, the exchange rate, and any fee. Visa says the choice must be yours. Declining conversion does not cancel your withdrawal. So choose dirhams, or the local currency, and let your card network and issuer handle conversion. Check your own card's fees too. Follow Market Debunk, and share this before someone's next trip.
