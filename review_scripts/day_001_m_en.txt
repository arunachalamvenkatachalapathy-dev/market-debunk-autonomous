At a jewellery counter, the gold necklace has a hallmark. Liya asks: "So the price must be fair?" Arun turns the bill over. The hallmark checks the metal's purity. It doesn't tell you what the whole necklace should cost. Look at the net weight of precious metal, its purity, and the separate charges on the bill. The Bureau of Indian Standards says these details belong on the invoice, including hallmarking charges. You can check the six-character HUID in the BIS Care app. Before paying, compare the full bill at more than one shop. Don't mistake a real hallmark for a good deal.
